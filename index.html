Hold a key to move the acid and bass into it: the middle row is the white notes, the row above the sharps. Hold several and each new note takes the next one, in the order you pressed them. Tap space while holding to lock it in; hold G and tap space to come home.

Click the graph to add or remove hits. On acid, click a note again for accent, again for slide. Space on its own plays.

Drag the graph up into the white bar to add it to the sequence. Click a graph there to edit it, drag it sideways to move it, or drag it off the bar to remove it.
