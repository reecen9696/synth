How it works

  1. Pick a style and press Make track. You get 8 patterns (A to H) and a song order.
  2. Press Play (or Space). Loop pattern repeats one bar, Play song runs the whole order.
  3. The grid is one bar of 16 steps. Each row is an instrument. Click a step to turn it on, drag to paint, Shift+click for an accent (louder).
  4. Click an instrument's name to open its sounds below. For melodic parts you get a note grid: click a row to pick the note, then mark accents and slides.
  5. M mutes, S solos. The steps number sets the loop length per track; odd lengths against 16 make polymeters.
  6. The Master filter is for live sweeps. Export WAV renders audio.

The maths

Every sound is a formula, shown under its controls with live values. Kicks are sines with an exponential pitch drop, hats are six square waves at the TR-808's clashing frequencies, the acid line is a saw through a resonant 24 dB lowpass with an exponential envelope, stabs and pads are detuned saws in chord shapes, plucks are two-operator FM. Euclidean rhythms spread k hits over n steps with (i·k) mod n < k.
